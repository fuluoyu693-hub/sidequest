How it would work

Three taps. No planning.

Set the mood

Cheap, quiet, outside, somewhere to sit down. One tap is enough.

Get one place

Not a ranked list. One pick, with the reason it chose that for you.

Say no, it learns

Every no is a clue. The next pick gets closer to what you actually like.

Why Sidequest

One answer, not a hundred.

Map and review apps

Show you many places near you. You still have to compare them and choose.

Trip planners

Build a whole itinerary before you leave. Plans change once you are on the street.

Sidequest

Picks one thing to do next, while you are standing there, and tells you why.

Is the app real?

No. Nothing is built. The demo runs on a short list of hand-written examples, not on a model or live data. We would rather say that than fake it.

What happens to my email?

We use it only to tell you about Sidequest, and to set up a chat if you ticked that box. We do not share it.

Who is building it?

A student team. No funding, no company. This page is a course project that tests whether the idea is worth building.
